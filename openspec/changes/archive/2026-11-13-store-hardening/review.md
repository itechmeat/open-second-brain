D7 items 4 and 5, a new requirement "Leaving fails closed without the shared store id" with its scenario in `held-partitions` (the refusal requirement now says every refusal changes nothing), task 1.1 case 10, task 1.5 steps 5 to 9 and the refusal type, task 2.1 case 9, task 2.2 refusal list, and both docs texts of task 2.4 updated.
