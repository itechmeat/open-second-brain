# Truth-correctable, time-aware claims - validity windows, windowed recall, grounded agent-stated claims, a correct verb, and guaranteed serve-with-correction

**Status:** draft
**Author:** feature-release orchestrator (via feature-release-playbook)
**Audience:** implementation

## Problem statement

The Open Second Brain truth ledger keys every comparison on assertion time: a 2024-dated claim ingested today still reads as a fresh contest against a 2026 claim, because `ClaimEvent` carries no validity window and the conflict scanner measures pure assertion-time distance. Recall cannot window claim events at all, agent-stated claims cannot ground into the ledger with per-claim verdicts, no correction verb exists anywhere, and no serving surface guarantees that a retired claim is served together with its correction. The multi-hop recall arm has no width bounds, no hub handling, no ordered path provenance, and nothing pins direct-hit precedence.

## Scope

- Optional per-claim validity fields (`validFrom`/`validUntil`) on `ClaimEvent`, `AppendClaimInput` and `ClaimVersion`, presence-gated under `TRUTH_SCHEMA_VERSION = 1`, with parsing reused from the existing validity discipline.
- A succession channel beside the conflicts array: a separate, optional `TruthState.successions` field, never a new conflict kind; ask_user semantics, hygiene findings and priority ranking untouched.
- Ingest window defaults: explicit input wins; otherwise ingest resolves the window from the source record's frontmatter validity when that source is readable at ingest, storing the frozen resolved value.
- A dedicated `events` operation on `brain_truth` (plus a CLI subcommand) with `since`/`until`/`entity`/`limit`, leaving today's `slots` response untouched entirely; rows carry validity fields verbatim and are reach-gated per row.
- Grounded agent-stated claims as ledger events with the `agent_stated` extractor tag and per-claim verdicts: grounded claims commit, ungrounded claims are reported back with reasons.
- A single `correct` verb (MCP action on `brain_lifecycle`, CLI subcommand on `brain lifecycle`) sweeping discovery, per-target retirement, mention retargeting and receipt bundling, with per-target end-state policy and a fail-recorded, idempotent, bundle-correlated receipt sequence.
- Deepened relational traversal with width caps (8 seeds, 4 per node, 16 total nodes), hub skipping by typed-edge degree, deadline bounding, entity-bridge edges over `chunk_entities`, and ordered per-node reach-gated path provenance.
- Structural direct-hit precedence with a pinning test reconciled against the promote-never-sink rerank pin.
- A shared serve-with-correction coupling predicate consumed by every serving surface (search pool and post-rank, recall by topic, context-pack, active.md, dream), fail-closed on unresolved or out-of-reach successors.
- Documentation updates (README, docs/architecture.md agent-scope-matrix equality, plugin mirrors via `bun run sync-plugin-mirrors`).

## Out of scope

- Claim-edge composition over grounded object-entity linking: deferred to the next wave. The contract names the future edge source (claim/entity edges derived from sub-suite A's grounded `agent_stated` events joined through the entity registry) and its enablement gate (grounded claims accumulated in live data, object-entity linking landed). Traversal over edges with no live data cannot earn its budget.
- Self-mined relation templates: the accept-gate design is its own card; no data exists until grounded claims accumulate.
- A tombstone/supersede op type in the write batch: the sweep runs as a fail-recorded sequence this wave, not as one Kernel 2 batch.
- A `--since-last-run` polling cursor on the truth ledger: tail-following stays consumer-side; ascending order plus `limit` gives a resumable slice.
- Provenance re-weighting of conflict priority: this wave the `agent_stated` tag is annotation only; it never changes which pairs contest and never re-ranks conflict priority.
- The assertion-time sibling consumers `collision.ts` (14-day convergence) and `grounding.ts` (30-day contest balance) stay assertion-keyed, documented at their constants.
- Turning the relational arm default-on: enablement stays `relationalArmEnabled` plus rrf fusion; byte-identical off behavior is preserved by construction.

## Chosen approach

Variant 3, Two Sub-Suites over a Pinned Contract, is accepted as recommended by the consultant with no orchestrator override. The wave splits into two sequenced sub-suites joined by a small, test-pinned contract. Sub-suite A (temporal truth) delivers the ledger validity fields with the succession channel, the windowed events surface, and grounded agent-stated claims as ledger events. Sub-suite B (correction and recall) delivers the correct verb, the deepened traversal with direct-hit precedence, and the coupling invariant, consuming A only through the contract. The contract pins exactly the three genuinely cross-card decisions - the axis precedence rule with the windowed event shape, the correction record-state policy, and the coupling-invariant statement - and nothing else. This keeps each sub-suite's blast radius bounded and reviewable as one unit while still delivering the wave's novel invariants that a five-narrow-cards reading would defer, and unlike a fully coupled kernel-first wave it does not concentrate ledger schema, write-path and five-surface serving risk in one release.

## Design decisions

1. **The assertion-versus-validity axis is contract item 1, stated once.** A same-slot pair (same canonical `entity` and `aspect`) classifies as succession, not contest, if and only if both claims carry present validity windows and the windows do not intersect. Everything else - either claim windowless, or windows intersecting - keeps today's assertion-time behavior verbatim: 30-day `CONFLICT_WINDOW_DAYS` distance, different-source requirement, `kind: "value_conflict"`, `resolution: "ask_user"`. An expired window never suppresses contestation on its own: a windowed claim whose window has passed still contests under the fall-through. Rationale: this preserves the pinned byte-identical windowless path (`conflicts.ts:83` returns the base fold unchanged; `tests/core/brain/truth/conflicts.test.ts` pins "without conflicts the state matches the base fold exactly" and the bit-identical neutral default), answers the validator's pinned semantic decision explicitly, and keeps succession outside ask_user semantics so hygiene findings and priority ranking stay untouched.

2. **Succession surfaces as a separate channel, not a conflict kind.** `TruthState` gains `readonly successions?: ReadonlyArray<ClaimSuccession>` - optional and presence-gated, set only when non-empty, serialized with the conditional-spread pattern from `store.ts:128-138` so every windowless line, fold, state file and conflict result stays byte-identical. `fold.ts` is untouched; the succession pass lives in the policy layer (`conflicts.ts` plus a new `succession.ts` sibling), matching the existing policy-over-fold shape. `TruthConflictKind` gains no member, so `ask_user` semantics, the hygiene detector and conflict priority are structurally unreachable from succession code. Rationale: a new conflict kind would drag succession into dispute machinery built for ask_user; an optional channel adds read-side tolerance for an absent field instead.

3. **Window defaults are explicit-input-first, frozen-at-ingest.** CLI flags `--valid-from`/`--valid-until` and declared optional MCP ingest fields win outright. Absent input, ingest resolves the window from the source record's frontmatter `valid_from`/`valid_until` when the source is readable at ingest (the first rung of the EventTimeResolver order; no mtime fallback - an mtime is an assertion-time proxy, not validity). The resolved value is stored frozen on the event; never a live reference. If the source is unreadable or windowless, the event is stored windowless and behaves byte-identically to today. Rationale: scan time never reads the vault, the ledger stays self-contained, and the freeze is contract text so A and B cannot reinterpret it.

4. **One interval convention inside the truth layer: half-open.** Ledger windows use `[validFrom, validUntil)` with `isValidAt` semantics from `lifecycle/temporal-replace.ts`, reusing `ValidityBounds`. Values parse with the `search/validity.ts` discipline (bare dates day-snapped, datetimes, never relative phrases) - no second grammar. The events operation's `since`/`until` are a different vocabulary: they filter assertion `ts` only, through the shared `time-range.ts` grammar with its inclusive day-edge semantics. Both schema descriptions state their convention explicitly so the two temporal vocabularies stay unambiguous inside one response.

5. **Windowed recall is a dedicated `events` operation, not a slots filter.** `brain_truth` gains `operation: "events"` with `entity` (optional, normalized, composing), `since`, `until`, `limit`; the CLI gains `o2b brain truth events` with the same arguments, matching the existing CLI-over-MCP op asymmetry (`sweep` precedent). Today's `slots` response is untouched entirely, so every existing consumer stays byte-identical by construction - `events` in the slots/conflicts responses remains the count pinned by `tests/mcp/brain-truth.test.ts:96`. The events response is `{ ok: true, operation: "events", entity: string | null, events: ClaimEventRow[], total: number, withheld: number, truncated: boolean }` where `ClaimEventRow` is the stored event with validity fields verbatim. Ordering is stable ascending assertion `ts` (the `readClaimEvents` order), which makes tail-follow pagination an advancing `since`. Default limit `DEFAULT_EVENT_LIST_LIMIT = 200`, hard cap `CLAIM_EVENT_MAX_LIST_LIMIT = 1000`, `truncated` set when more rows matched. Bounds are resolved through the shared grammar and floored/ceiled to the second (`eventsWindowBounds` in the new `events-window.ts`), exact against second-precision stamps via string compare. Every row passes the same per-row visibility/reach gate `brain_claims` uses; rows dropped by the gate are counted in `withheld` so a remote caller sees deterministic output and a withheld page stays indistinguishable from an absent one. Rationale: this is the corrected mechanics from the premise verification - `events` as a name is taken by the count, and the card's "filter the returned events" reading was false because no events were ever returned.

6. **Agent-stated claims land as ledger events with per-claim verdicts.** A stated claim is `{ subject, relation, object }` plus assertion `text`, `source` and optional `agent`. It commits as one `ClaimEvent` with `entity` = normalized subject, `aspect` = the relation token (validated against the single relation vocabulary boundary, `isKnownRelation`), `value` = object display form, and `extractor: "agent_stated"` (new optional field on `ClaimEvent`/`AppendClaimInput`, tolerated by `coerceClaim` under schema v1). Grounding uses the one existing occurrence kernel: a pure `anchorEntityForms` export lifted from `atomic-facts.ts` (quality-gated normalized match forms, minimum length 3); subject and object must be anchored in `text` or the claim is reported back, not committed. Naming deliberately avoids "grounding", which is taken by the source-diversity score in `truth/grounding.ts`; the new module and verdicts speak of anchoring. Refuse-before-write still governs the payload boundary: an unknown relation, missing text or missing source refuses the whole call with nothing written; per-claim partial commit applies only to the anchoring verdict, which is safe because each event is an independent append in an append-only ledger and the response reports exactly what landed - the first partial-commit lane in this subsystem, justified by the verdict contract. Rationale: this is the store mapping that makes provenance visible to conflict detection without touching `contestingValues`; the tag is additive annotation on conflict rows this wave.

7. **The correction end-state policy is contract item 2, one pure function.** `src/core/brain/truth/correction-policy.ts` pins: `correctionEndState(input: { flatlyWrong: boolean; windowEnd?: string }, correctionTs: string): { endState: "validity_close" | "tombstone"; validUntil: string | null }`. A flatly-wrong claim tombstones (hidden; the coupling invariant is vacuous for it, which is correct - nothing surfaces, correction included). Every other correction closes validity at `windowEnd` when time-scoped, else at the correction instant: the predecessor stays serveable inside its historical window, which is the only regime where serve-with-correction is non-trivial. The choice is recorded per target in the bundle receipt using the existing receipt reason codes (`supersede` for validity_close, `tombstone` for tombstone - name-aligned CLI verb, MCP action, receipt code). Succession logic consumes the policy's outcome through the ledger data (a validity-closed predecessor against its successor has non-overlapping present windows and classifies as succession under contract item 1); path-provenance annotation reads the same frontmatter outcome. Rationale: the recon showed `supersede()`-only sweeps make coupling vacuous for their own outputs because tombstoned means hidden; per-target choice keeps both regimes honest and answers the fork the card never surfaced.

8. **The sweep is validate-all dry-run, then fail-recorded, no batch op.** `brain_lifecycle correct` (MCP action) and `o2b brain lifecycle correct` (CLI verb) gather the affected set within the caller's reach: the claim-graph closure (`whatReplaced` chain tip plus `whatContests`), a full-vault match-only wikilink mention scan via `retargetWikilinks` with `apply: false` (the counting pass asserts no vault identity), and same-entity/aspect truth-ledger claims. Dry-run returns the blast-radius report and writes nothing. Applied runs a fail-recorded sequence reusing `retargetWikilinks`' per-write failure carry: per-target retirement through the end-state policy, mention retargeting, per-record receipts via `appendDecisionChangeReceipt` with the bundle id carried in `evidence_triggers` as `correction_bundle:<bundleId>` (the receipt schema stays closed). For ledger claims, the correction appends a new event asserting the corrected value from the successor's source - same-source value change reads as self-correction in the fold (`conflicts.ts:36-44`), never a conflict. Replay converges: tombstone is a byte-identical no-op on replay, receipts return `appended: false`, an already-closed window stays closed. Brain/log and receipt lines are never rewritten (testimony). Rationale: fail-recorded matches the `retargetWikilinks` precedent and avoids a first-of-kind batch op type in the same wave that introduces the underlying data.

9. **The coupling invariant is contract item 3, one predicate, every surface.** `src/core/search/correction-coupling.ts` pins `couplingVerdict(input: { predecessorPath: string; successorPath: string | null; successorReadable: boolean }): CouplingVerdict` with `CouplingVerdict = { action: "serve_coupled"; correctionPath: string } | { action: "drop" }`. A retired-but-serveable row (validity-closed predecessor inside its window, or superseded non-tip still serveable) is served only when its chain-tip correction is resolved and readable at the caller's reach; otherwise the row is dropped fail-closed, so a withheld page stays indistinguishable from an absent one. Content pulled in as a correction re-runs the full filter set - status, visibility scope, agent scope, reach - closing the recorded gap at `post-rank.ts:142-168` where polarity-pulled successors bypass visibility and agent scope. The predicate replaces the search pipeline's best-effort polarity branch for retired rows and is consumed by recall by topic, context-pack, active.md and dream scan, replacing today's hide-or-annotate split with one rule. Tombstoned rows stay dropped by the status filter everywhere; the predicate governs the serveable-retired regime only. Rationale: the recon verified the partial coupling exists in search only, is best-effort, and is void for tombstoned predecessors - the genuinely missing property is the guarantee across surfaces, which needs exactly one predicate to be testable.

10. **Traversal budgets are named constants with config override; hub is degree, never prose.** `relationalFanout` gains `TRAVERSAL_MAX_SEEDS = 8`, `TRAVERSAL_MAX_EXPANSION_PER_NODE = 4`, `TRAVERSAL_MAX_TOTAL_NODES = 16`, and hub detection: a node whose walked-edge degree (typed edges plus entity bridges) exceeds `TRAVERSAL_HUB_DEGREE_THRESHOLD = 12` is reached but not expanded. All four are overridable through `OPEN_SECOND_BRAIN_SEARCH_TRAVERSAL_*` config following the `parseBool(envOrConfig(...))` pattern. The walk stays deadline-bounded by accepting a deadline parameter; on a fired clock the frontier is abandoned deterministically and already-reached nodes are kept. Depth stays 2 by default. Rationale: the arm today has no width bound at all - every query wikilink is a seed and every typed edge of every frontier document is followed; upstream's 8/4/16 budgets are the copied precedent, and deadline bounding is required because recall phases run under a composite deadline.

11. **Entity bridges from chunk_entities, behind their own flag.** A new store reader `entityBridgesForDocuments` (new file `src/core/search/store/entity-bridges.ts`) joins the `chunk_entities` table so two documents mentioning one normalized entity get a bridge edge labeled `entity`. Bridges are walked by the deepened arm when enabled (`OPEN_SECOND_BRAIN_SEARCH_ENTITY_BRIDGES`, default true, read only when the arm itself is enabled); they are machine-derived, not part of the query edge-type vocabulary, so `parseRelationalQuery` validation is unaffected and attribution renders them as `via entity`. Rationale: this is the chunk_entities half of the corrected scope - document typed relations plus entity co-occurrence now, while claim-edge composition over grounded linking waits for live data.

12. **Direct-hit precedence is structural admission plus a tightened rerank pin.** Relational-only rows (absent from the organic candidate set) are admitted after the last organic row in the pre-rerank fused order, and the rerank pin for relational-origin rows is tightened: a relational-origin row may move within the relational block and may never sink below its pre-rerank position relative to other relational rows, and may never cross above an organic direct hit. The two pins coexist and are both test-pinned. Rationale: the recon showed nothing pins precedence today - neutral RRF weight 1 lets a relational-only rank-1 row outrank a keyword-rank-20 direct hit - and a sub-neutral lane weight would perturb all fusion globally, while admission is local to the arm's rows and reconciles cleanly with promote-never-sink.

13. **Ordered path provenance is per-node reach-gated and frontmatter-annotated.** `RelationalReach` gains an ordered path of `{ documentId, relation }` steps; only nodes readable at the caller's reach appear (per-node gating, the `brain_derive_fact` premise-gate precedent), and the attribution reason renders withheld hops as a count (`relational: via <types> (<n> hops, <k> nodes withheld)`). The machine-readable ordered path lands in the typed retrieval-trail vocabulary as a new stable code carrying readable document ids plus the withheld count - additive reason strings stay human provenance, the trail stays typed. When a readable path node is a non-tip superseded predecessor whose window has closed, the row is annotated `superseded_by: <tip>` through the record-state policy's frontmatter outcome - search reads frontmatter only, never the ledger, keeping the search-to-ledger coupling at zero.

14. **The arm's enablement story does not change.** The deepened traversal rides the existing `relationalArmEnabled` (default false) plus rrf-fusion gates. Byte-identical off behavior holds by construction: with the arm off, fusion, ranking, attribution and the coupling predicate's search wiring see unchanged inputs. Rationale: flipping a default-on in the same wave as the coupling change would silently re-rank every stock deployment; enablement is an explicit operator choice documented by the docs lane.

15. **Schema-version discipline: optional fields stay under version 1.** `validFrom`, `validUntil` and `extractor` are optional, presence-gated fields tolerated by `coerceClaim` and serialized by conditional spread. The read/write matrix: a new binary reads old lines (fields absent) and new lines (fields present, validated when present); an old binary reads new lines, ignores unknown keys, and behaves on the assertion-time axis exactly as today - state files carry `successions` only when non-empty, and old binaries' strict state validators treat the derived cache as recomputable (`readTruthState` reads null on structural mismatch and the caller refolds from events). No version bump, no migration.

16. **One operator concept `correct`, no new MCP tool.** The verb extends `brain_lifecycle` with a `correct` action and the CLI lifecycle verb with a `correct` subcommand. The agent-scope-matrix equality keeps its tool count and gains the recipe rows for the new action; the test equality and docs/architecture.md classification move together (implementation lane updates the test, docs lane updates the document and regenerates mirrors). Rationale: a new `brain_correct` tool would grow the matrix tool count for a concept that is lifecycle retirement plus provenance, which `brain_lifecycle` already owns.

## Risks and open questions

- **Contract drift.** The contract is new surface; a weak or stale contract would let the sub-suites drift exactly where they must not. Mitigation: all three items are pure modules with named test files committed by lane 1 and lane 3 first, and the integrator owns the cross-suite pinning test `tests/contract/serve-with-correction.test.ts`.
- **Partial-commit precedent.** Per-claim commit for stated claims is the first partial-commit write lane in the subsystem. The verdict contract keeps it honest: the payload boundary still refuses whole calls, only anchoring verdicts are per-claim, and the response reports exactly what landed.
- **Entity-bridge noise.** chunk_entities bridges can connect popular entities across unrelated documents. Mitigation: hub skipping by degree, width caps, and a dedicated flag to disable bridges independent of the arm.
- **Forward/backward compatibility.** Old binaries reading new lines ignore unknown keys and stay on the assertion-time axis; a mixed-device vault therefore shows axis behavior per device until all devices upgrade. Acceptable for a derived, recomputable cache and append-only events; noted here so it is a documented property, not a surprise.
- **Sweep blast radius.** A wrong target selection could retire many records. Mitigation: dry-run-first is the default UX, retarget is match-only and never rewrites code spans or Brain/log, receipts are per-record and idempotent, and the counting pass asserts no vault identity.
- **Open during implementation.** The exact hub threshold value (12) and the entity-bridge default may need tuning against a real corpus; both are named constants with config override so tuning does not change code shape. The retrieval-trail code name for the ordered path is finalized in review against the existing stable-code vocabulary.

## Premise verification

Verdicts below come from the premise-verification recon reports, which are authoritative over the card bodies. The recons confirmed two cards as stated and corrected three; each corrected card's scope in this design follows the corrected scope, not the card text.

### t_edba0c15 - Per-claim temporal validity windows feeding conflict-vs-succession: CORRECTED

The gap is real but narrower and differently seated than the card implies: the conflict scanner genuinely has no validity axis and keys purely on assertion time (ingest-order dependence confirmed reproducible), but per-claim windows, a point-in-time query and a succession-aware claims view already exist on the sibling claim-graph layer, and the card's claims-table-view half is already at parity (`brain_claims` / `o2b brain claims` already render windows, provenance, supersession and tombstone status). Corrected scope adopted: (a) optional `validFrom`/`validUntil` on `ClaimEvent`/`AppendClaimInput`/`ClaimVersion` with presence-gated serialization under schema v1; (b) window defaults resolved from the source record's event time, frozen at ingest; (c) the conflict policy layer extended so two same-slot claims with non-overlapping present windows classify as succession while the windowless path stays byte-identical and succession stays outside ask_user semantics; (d) the precedence rule stated explicitly as contract item 1. The claims-table half is dropped as present parity; the one missing extractor column belongs to t_3e87656b's `agent_stated` tag.

### t_cb7ff437 - Windowed entity-fact recall: since/until on brain_truth slots: CORRECTED

The gap is real (no since/until anywhere on the truth-ledger recall surface; the strict grammar is reusable), but the card's central reading was false: the slots response has never carried claim-event rows - `events` in that response is a count, pinned by test - so the feature necessarily extends the response shape or adds an operation, and the validator's either/or ("filter the events fed to the fold" versus "filter the returned events") is moot. Corrected scope adopted: a dedicated `operation: "events"` on `brain_truth` plus a CLI subcommand, leaving today's slots response untouched entirely so existing consumers stay byte-identical by construction; stable ascending order, named default limit, inclusive day-edge semantics inherited from the shared grammar, rows reach-gated per row, validity fields passed through verbatim, and the schema description stating that since/until filter assertion `ts` only. The fold stays whole: conflicts and current truth keep being computed over the full event set.

### t_c37af4e9 - Multi-hop recall across claim/entity chains with bounded fan-out and path provenance: CORRECTED

The validator's resolution is confirmed - a bounded multi-hop lane exists and the card is a present_weaker extension of it - with two parity fixes the design respects: the relational arm is doubly opt-in (default-off and rrf-only, so unconditionally extending it ships to no one), and direct-hit precedence is NOT an existing guardrail (neutral RRF weight 1 lets a relational-only row outrank a direct hit; no test pins precedence). Corrected scope adopted: width caps (8 seeds, 4 per node, 16 total) and hub skipping by typed-edge degree as new work with named constants and config override; direct-hit precedence as a new structural mechanism (admission below the last organic row plus a tightened rerank pin) with a pinning test; ordered-path provenance with per-node reach gating; traversal over document typed relations plus chunk_entities entity bridges now; claim-edge composition over grounded object-entity linking deferred to the next wave behind the enablement sub-suite A ships, with the contract naming the future edge source and its gate; the arm's default-off enablement unchanged.

### t_cabe84aa - Single correct-this-fact verb with served-only-with-correction coupling: CONFIRMED

Confirmed: the preservation half is at parity as claimed, no single correction verb exists anywhere in src/ or tests/, and no serving surface guarantees a retired claim is served coupled with its correction. Two refinements verified and folded in: a partial best-effort coupling already exists in the search pipeline only (relation polarity demotes a non-tombstoned predecessor and pulls in a resolved successor; unresolved leaves the predecessor served bare; topic recall, context-pack, active.md and dream have no analogue), and the `trust.replacement` pointer is opt-in, not unconditional. The design's novelty is the guaranteed cross-surface coupling (contract item 3) and the per-target end-state policy (contract item 2) the card never surfaced: supersede-only sweeps make coupling vacuous for their own outputs because tombstoned means hidden.

### t_3e87656b - Agent-stated claims with mechanical grounding, provenance-based conflict detection, self-mined relation templates: CONFIRMED

Confirmed: all three gaps are real (no occurrence-grounded declared-claim path, no provenance-aware conflict detection, no relation-template learning). One inventory correction adopted: the closer existing path is the typed extraction intake (`extract-intake.ts`), not only `derived-fact.ts`, and the one existing occurrence-in-text kernel is `anchorEntities` in `atomic-facts.ts` - both are the extension points this design uses. Scope within this wave: agent-stated claims land as ledger events with the `agent_stated` extractor tag and per-claim anchoring verdicts; provenance is annotation only and never changes which pairs contest (the `ask_user` resolution stays pinned); the intake-relation bridge is contract-named and deferred with object-entity linking; relation-template learning is out of scope as its own future accept-gated card. The naming hazard is respected: "grounding" is taken by the source-diversity score in `truth/grounding.ts`, so the new verdicts speak of anchoring.
